---
title: "Customize your widget"
description: "Tell the widget agent what your widget must do, and the agent changes the widget's code. Tolstoy widgets have no JavaScript SDK."
---

Each Tolstoy widget has its own code. To change what a widget does, you do not call a JavaScript API from your theme. You tell the widget agent what you want, and the agent changes the widget's code.

The agent can connect the widget to the code of your store: your cart drawer, your quick view, your buttons, and your analytics.

## Change a widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    In Tolstoy, go to **Widgets** and open the widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell the agent what you want">
    Write your request in the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Examine the preview">
    The agent shows the result in **Widget Preview**. The **Code** tab shows the code. Ask for more changes until the widget does what you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. Your store does not change until you publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Write a good request

- Write what the shopper does and what must happen. For example: "When a shopper clicks Add to cart, open my cart drawer."
- Give the URL of a page where the widget shows. The agent can open your store in a browser and examine its code.
- If you know the function, element, or event that your theme uses, give its name. Then the agent does not have to find it.
- If mobile and desktop must be different, write what each one must do.

## Examples

Replace the names in these examples with the names that your store uses.

### Use your cart drawer

```text
When a shopper clicks Add to cart, add the item through my theme's cart and open my cart drawer.
My theme adds items with window.theme.cart.addItem(variantId, quantity) and opens the drawer with window.theme.cart.open().
```

### Open your quick view

```text
When a shopper clicks a product, open my theme's quick view for that product instead of the Tolstoy product details.
My quick view opens when a "quickview:open" event with the product URL in its detail bubbles up from the page.
```

### Open the player from your button

```text
On product pages, open the videos in full screen when a shopper clicks my "Watch videos" button (id="watch-videos").
```

### Close the player when your cart opens

```text
When my cart drawer opens, close the full-screen player so that the shopper sees the cart.
```

### Send events to your analytics

```text
Push an event to my Google Tag Manager dataLayer when a shopper plays a video, clicks a product, or adds to cart.
Name the events tolstoy_video_play, tolstoy_product_click and tolstoy_add_to_cart.
```

### Show the widget only on some pages

```text
Show the bubble only on collection pages, and only after the shopper scrolls down half of the page.
```

### Match your store design

```text
Use my store's fonts and button style. Make the product cards look like the product cards on this page:
https://your-store.com/collections/all
```
