- **[MCP](/mcp/overview)**: connect ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Codex to your Tolstoy library and to Tolstoy Studio.
- **Storefront**: [customize your widget](/storefront/customize-your-widget) with the widget agent, and control [cookie consent](/storefront/cookie-consent) and the [chat assistant](/storefront/chat-assistant) from your site code.

Do you need something that we do not support? Tell us at [support@gotolstoy.com](mailto:support@gotolstoy.com).
