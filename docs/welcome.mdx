- **[Tolstoy for AI agents](/agents/overview)**: connect ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Codex to one Tolstoy MCP server, which reads your brand, products and media and creates images and videos.
- **[CLI](/agents/cli)**: work with your workspace from a terminal, a script, or an AI coding agent.
- **Storefront**: [install Tolstoy](/storefront/installation) on your site, [customize your widget](/storefront/customize-your-widget) with the widget agent, and control [cookie consent](/storefront/cookie-consent) and the [chat assistant](/storefront/chat-assistant) from your site code.

Do you need something that we do not support? Tell us at [support@gotolstoy.com](mailto:support@gotolstoy.com).
